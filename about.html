Vivek Shanti Vishaal

Hindustani Classical Vocalist & Composer

Vivek Shanti Vishaal is a young dynamic Hindustani classical vocalist from Prayagraj, groomed under famous Dhrupad maestro Pt. Prem Kumar Mallik of Darbhanga Gharana. He has many students learning online and offline in India and abroad too. He is Sangit Praveen, Sangeet Nipun and holds a Master’s in Music from the University of Allahabad. Vivek, who is a JRF scholar, is also a music composer and is currently pursuing his PhD from the University of Allahabad.

He is the recipient of the Scholarship Award for Khyal Gayan by the Ministry of Culture (2006–2008) and the state winner of Khyal Gayan (2007), organised by Uttar Pradesh Sangeet Natak Akademi, Lucknow. He also leads a team of vocalists who sing Kabeerwani in their distinct style.
